# Home page brief

Scope: home page (src/App.tsx). Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: families and groups from Luanda on a phone, usually arriving from WhatsApp. Job: decide on a weekend or day at the Mussulo. Action: start a WhatsApp booking conversation. Proof: the client's real photos only. Constraints: no prices, capacities or claims the resort has not stated.
Memorable moment: walking the boardwalk; the booking pad that writes the WhatsApp message for you.
