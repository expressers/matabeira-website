# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + TypeScript + Vite, Bun, Tailwind CSS v4 + shadcn/ui (customised), deployed as a static site on Render. Same stack as the resort's digital menu (expressers/matabeira-menu). Chosen by Eládio.

## Users

Families and groups from Luanda planning a weekend or day escape to the Mussulo peninsula. They browse on the phone, usually from a WhatsApp link or social media, and decide quickly with whoever they are going with.

## Product Purpose

Resort Matabeira's website. The home page must make a Luanda family want to spend the weekend there and get them talking to the resort on WhatsApp to book. Success = WhatsApp booking conversations started.

## Positioning

A resort on the Mussulo with its own beach, pool, restaurant and bar in one place, white chalets with red tile roofs along wooden walkways, and a kitchen that serves Cuban, Angolan and international food. Tagline in use: "O seu refúgio no Mussulo".

## Operating Context

Mussulo is reached from Luanda by boat or by road along the peninsula. Visitors come for the day or stay over. The resort also hosts weddings (stated by the client).

## Capabilities and Constraints

Stated by the resort (WhatsApp, Oct 2026): piscina, praia, restaurante, bar, música 24 h, salão de jogos e sala de fumadores; comida cubana, angolana e internacional; weddings.
Reservations and information: 927 648 082 and 942 236 127 (from the printed menu). WhatsApp booking uses 927 648 082.
Undecided / unknown, never invent: accommodation types, capacity, prices, check-in rules, exact address, opening hours, social media handles, email.
Home page only for now.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Resort Matabeira" / "Matabeira Resort". Tagline "O seu refúgio no Mussulo". The client shared a logo concept (palms, sun and waves; green, orange and blue) that is a watermarked Design.com mockup: it is reference only and must not ship as-is. The digital menu (matabeira-menu.onrender.com) uses dark wood, gold and palm green. Language: European/Angolan Portuguese.

## Evidence on Hand

Photos sent by the client (in _raw/, from WhatsApp, ~1200x1600 portrait): 1 beach with thatched umbrellas, 2 grilled fish plate, 3 breakfast, 4 beach with swings, 5 pool at night with thatched parasols, 6 sea/lagoon, 7 wooden walkway between chalets, 8 chalets with palm tree seen from the stairs, 9 logo mockup (reference only). No testimonials, ratings, press or awards exist: do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

- Show the place, not adjectives: real photos carry the persuasion.
- One action everywhere: talk to the resort on WhatsApp.
- Never invent prices, capacities or claims the resort has not stated.
- Mobile first: the visitor arrives from WhatsApp on a phone.
